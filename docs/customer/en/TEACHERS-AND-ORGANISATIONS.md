# Hlasuj! — teachers, organisations and languages

Development guide for the unreleased work dated 8 October 2026. These features are not established by the qualified 0.8.9 production release. Central AUTH login and live licence enforcement still require integration and acceptance evidence.

## Your teaching space

Use your own teacher account. In **Organisations and sharing**, select your personal teaching space or an organisation you belong to. New content belongs to the selected space, and lists show the content you can access there. Existing content remains with its original author in the personal space; creating an organisation does not move it automatically.

The installation administrator maintains the whole WordPress installation. An organisation administrator manages their own organisation and gains no WordPress administrator privileges.

## Working with colleagues

Select your subject, enter the username of an existing teacher account and choose **Collaborating teacher** or **Read only**. Within an organisation, the recipient must also be a member. Sharing a subject includes its lectures and their attached questions in the same space.

A collaborating teacher can edit and control shared teaching. They cannot reshare or transfer someone else's objects. A reader can view shared content and results, but cannot start teaching, edit content or delete test results. Removing one grant leaves any other valid grants intact. Removing organisation membership revokes access to that organisation without deleting its content.

Owners can transfer content to an eligible teacher in the same organisation. Permanent links and QR codes stay unchanged. Transferring a question does not transfer the subject or remove permissions inherited through a subject or lecture. Review all grants when handing over teaching.

## Choosing a language

Students use **Čeština / English**; their browser remembers the choice. Teachers use their WordPress profile language. The language setting changes controls and application messages. It does not translate your question titles, answers, explanations, notes or participant nicknames.

Content export/import preserves the original text and does not copy accounts, organisation membership or permissions. The standalone web demo includes prepared English variants of its sample questions; your own teaching content stays in its original language.

## Privacy and licences

Review private notes and explanations before sharing a subject or its exported file. Student and results links retain their established public behaviour. Teacher access controls do not make a public results link private.

The confirmed licence rule counts each distinct account with teaching rights once across the organisation. Students and readers do not consume a teaching seat. Local membership is not proof of a central licence; live AUTH and licensing integration must be qualified before this is presented as a complete integrated release.
