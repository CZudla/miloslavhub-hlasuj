# Hlasuj! by MiloslavHub

## Invite everyone into the lesson

Hlasuj! supports live quizzes and polls on students' own devices. Share a permanent QR code, open the question when you are ready, and discuss the results together.

- Students join in a browser without installing an application or creating a student account.
- Teachers control the live voting flow.
- Quizzes can use correct answers, time limits and scoring; polls collect opinions without quiz points.
- Projection views show the current question and results.
- Reusable subject and lecture content can be transferred using portable files with an import preview.
- Optional AI suggestions require an explicit teacher action and review.

## Try the demonstration

The standalone demo uses temporary, separate data and labels its 17 fictional respondents. Its automatic sequence lets visitors try the student experience. A real live lesson remains under teacher control.

## Development preview, 8 October 2026

The current development branch adds local teacher accounts, organisation roles, explicit sharing and an expanded Czech/English interface. These additions require release qualification and are not represented here as deployed capabilities of the qualified 0.8.9 baseline.

Central AUTH login and live organisational licence enforcement remain dependent on qualified upstream integrations. The confirmed seat policy counts each distinct account with teaching rights once across the organisation; students and read-only accounts do not consume a teaching seat. Homework is outside this iteration.
